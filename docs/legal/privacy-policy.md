Lonagram is a social app for adults (18+). Members get a private "Twin" (an AI that talks for them) that can talk with other members' Twins so people can find shared interests before deciding to chat for real. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It covers the Lonagram website and the iOS and Android apps, which share one account.

## 1. What we collect

**You give us**
- Account details: username, display name, sign-in email or recovery email, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider, never readable by us), confirmation that you are 18 or older.
- Profile details: bio, city, languages, interests, intentions (friendship, networking, dating), profile photo.
- Twin details: personality, topics, hobbies, communication style, what you are looking for, and the notes ("memories") your Twin keeps from your private chats with it.
- Dating preferences, if you turn dating on: age, preferred age range, location scope, relationship goals, values.
- Messages: chats with your own Twin, chats between Twins, and direct messages with people you connected with.
- Reports and blocks you make, and anything you write in a report.

**Collected automatically**
- Technical data needed to run the service: account ID, sign-in sessions, request logs, rate-limit counters, and AI usage counts per account.
- The apps store your sign-in session in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore). We do not use advertising identifiers and do not run third-party advertising or analytics trackers in the app.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or microphone audio. The app asks for photo library access only when you choose a profile picture.

## 2. How we use it

- To run your account, profile, Twin, Discover suggestions, connections and messages.
- To generate Twin replies and to extract short notes from your private Twin chat. Your private notes stay private unless you choose to share them. Notes about sensitive topics (for example health, religion, politics, money, relationships) need an extra confirmation before they can be shared.
- To keep people safe: rate limiting, abuse prevention, reviewing reports, enforcing our Terms.
- To email you account messages (confirmation and password-reset links). We do not send marketing email unless you ask.

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Who can see what

- **Other members** see only what your profile and Twin permissions allow. You control which profile fields your Twin may share.
- When you chat with another member's Twin, that member can read the conversation. Discover tells you this before you start.
- A real chat opens only when both people agree to connect.
- Your private chat with your own Twin and its private notes are visible only to you (and to our systems that process them, see section 4).

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account (Settings, Delete account, in the app or on the website) we delete your profile, Twin, notes, connections and messages from our systems.
